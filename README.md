
# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

🚀 **Project Type:** Data Analytics | Excel + Power BI
🎯 **Objective:** Identify key drivers of customer churn and recommend strategies to improve retention

---

## 🧠 Problem Statement

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for subscription-based businesses.

This project analyzes customer data to answer:

* Why are customers leaving?
* Which segments are most at risk?
* How can churn be reduced using data-driven decisions?

---

## 📂 Dataset

* Telco Customer Churn Dataset
* ~7,000+ customer records
* Includes demographics, contract type, charges, and churn status

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies

* 📊 Excel (Data Cleaning & Preprocessing)
* 📈 Power BI (Dashboard & Visualization)

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Churn by Contract Type

* Compared churn rates across:

  * Month-to-month
  * One-year
  * Two-year

👉 Insight: Short-term contracts show significantly higher churn

---

### 2. Churn by Customer Segment

* New Customers
* Mid-term Customers
* Long-term Customers

👉 Insight: New customers are most likely to churn early

---

### 3. Churn by Charges

* Low, Medium, High spending groups

👉 Insight: High charges are associated with higher churn risk

## 🎯 Key Insights

* 📌 Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate
* 📌 Long-term contracts significantly improve retention
* 📌 High-paying customers are more sensitive to pricing
* 📌 Early-stage customers require better onboarding

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* ✅ Promote long-term contracts with discounts
* ✅ Improve onboarding experience for new customers
* ✅ Introduce loyalty programs
* ✅ Optimize pricing strategy for high-value users
